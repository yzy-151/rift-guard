# Starfall assets

`moonwell-garden.png` is a new background generated using the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-03 for this project. Original characters and enemies are referenced directly from the existing `assets/characters/sequences` and `assets/enemies/sequences` folders; no edits were made to those images.

The map is a static painted environment. The floating core, motes, runes, projectiles, actors, hit sparks and warning shapes are drawn and animated by `arena.gd`. `icon.svg` and blessing emblems are original code-authored vectors.

## ImageGen prompt

Create a production-ready 2D game environment texture for a polished cute fantasy action roguelite called Rift Guard, no text, no UI, no characters, no monsters. A very wide landscape 3:2 orthographic top-down slightly isometric moonlit abandoned observatory garden arena, a large open walkable slate-blue cobblestone plaza in the center occupying 75% of the image with subtle worn concentric astronomical engravings and tiny grass in cracks, restrained subdued contrast in the central playable floor so bright sprites read well. A shallow small circular moonwell stone dais at exact center. Around the distant perimeter only: sculpted teal fir trees, rounded indigo shrubs, tiny pastel blue and lilac flowers, ancient broken marble arches with brass crescent moon ornament, cozy warm amber lanterns and small observatory instruments. Bottom perimeter soft ferns and small mushrooms. Hand-painted high quality indie game background, beautiful deliberate shapes, soft painterly brush texture, rich navy and teal shadows, muted blue stone, lavender accents and delicate gold points of light. Moody but welcoming and whimsical, intricate framing around calm spacious flat central arena. View is almost straight down like Hades or Cult of the Lamb game environments, no horizon, no sky. Every location on the central plaza is open and unobstructed. Avoid beams of light, bloom fog, overbright floor, photorealism, realism, pixel art, heavy outlines. This will be used as actual map background with animated characters rendered separately. Maximum useful landscape resolution.

# Mode 1 V7 — persistent unlocks and player-controlled squads

- Clearing a stage now reveals its newly unlocked character before the next mission.
- A Helltaker-styled squad screen lists all eight characters, their element, role, attack, range, speed, and block count.
- The Traveler is mandatory and the player may bring up to two unlocked companions.
- The confirmed squad is carried into the next stage while all temporary cards and the Traveler element reset.
- Locked characters remain visible as progression goals and become selectable from the persistent compendium save.
- The final stage unlocks Keqing, Ganyu, Venti, Zhongli, and Bailu; F3 allows replaying any stage with a new unlocked squad.
- Every companion now has distinct epic and legendary mechanics, including explosive multishot, healing, chain lightning, frost bursts, piercing arrows, fortified cleave, and rapid healing.
- Rapid kills build a timed streak with milestone impact feedback, while cosmetic event limits keep late-game swarms responsive.
- The Excel workbook now includes every Mode 1 story line, twenty additional dialogue rows, and editable controls for the squad screen.

Validation screenshots are generated in `docs/v7-validation` by the `--v7-test` run.

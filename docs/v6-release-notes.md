# Mode 1 V6 — elemental builds, specialist swarms, and stage three

- The card pool now contains 152 cards, including six unlimited-stack build cards for every Traveler element.
- Elemental reactions now support directional Vaporize and Melt multipliers, full Freeze, Shatter, spreading Swirl, Overload knockback, Superconduct weakening, Electro-Charged chaining, and shared Crystallize shielding.
- Four enemy roles join the roster: Charger, Healer, Splitter, and Warder.
- A third six-minute stage adds a distinct Helltaker background, shifted three-lane route, a full three-character squad, and the summoning boss Black Obsidian Broodmother.
- All three stages mix specialist enemies into increasingly dense late-game swarms.
- Helltaker UI, story presentation, transition animation, buttons, backgrounds, and interface sounds remain active across every screen.

Validation screenshots are generated in `docs/v6-validation` by the `--v6-test` run.
